1. Executive Summary Vision Create the world's first fullyAI-automated recruitment platform, beginning with UK health and social care sector, with expansion to education, hospitality, and administrative roles. The platform will compete directly with Indeed, Reed, and CV Library while offering superior AI-powered features. Business Models B2C: Job seekers access jobs for free B2B: Employers pay to post jobs and access candidate database B2BSaaS:White-label platform sold to otherrecruitment agencies 、 CoreDifferentiators AI-powered job matching and candidate screening AutomatedinterviewschedulingwithAI bots Real-time compliance tracking for CQC requirements Voice cloning forinterviewbots Intelligent candidate recommendations Comprehensive document management vault Multi-tenant SaaS architecture Success Metrics Page load time: <3 seconds First Contentful Paint:<1.5 seconds Lighthouse Performance Score: >90 Mobile usability: 100% WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance Conversion rate (job application): >5% User registration rate:>15% of visitors 3lPage
